A day at Harmony

Gentle rhythms, familiar routines

No two days are identical, because they follow each resident. Here's what a typical day can look like.

Morning

Unhurried wake-ups, help with washing and dressing, morning medications and a warm breakfast.

Midday

Lunch at the table, conversation, music or a favorite show, and rest for those who like a nap.

Afternoon

Visits from family, games, puzzles or porch time, plus snacks and plenty of fluids.

Evening & night

Dinner, evening care and a calm bedtime, with caregivers awake and nearby all night.

Comfort & safety

Designed to feel safe, not clinical

  • Single-level living with no stairs between bedroom, bathroom and living areas
  • Ramp entrance with railings at the front door
  • Walk-in shower with grab bars and a handheld shower head
  • Gated back deck with safety railings
  • Caregivers on site 24/7, including overnight
  • Emergency and evacuation plan for every resident, as Washington requires
  • Open common areas where staff can keep a gentle eye on everyone
  • Clean, bright rooms that residents are encouraged to personalize
