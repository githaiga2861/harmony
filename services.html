Care & services

Everything your loved one needs, under one roof

From daily living assistance to total care, our services are built around one person at a time and adjusted whenever needs change.

What we provide

Ten ways we care every day

Every resident receives the services written into their personal care plan. Here is what that can include.

Personal Care & Daily Living

Respectful, hands-on help with the activities of daily living.

  • Bathing, showering and grooming
  • Dressing and oral care
  • Toileting and continence care
  • Safe transfers and walking support

Medication Management

Medications handled carefully and consistently.

  • Given on schedule as prescribed
  • Daily medication records
  • Refills coordinated with your pharmacy
  • Changes communicated to family

Health Monitoring & Coordination

We keep everyone on the same page about health.

  • Routine vital signs and weight
  • Doctor appointment scheduling
  • Updates to physicians and family
  • Nurse delegation when required

Meals & Nutrition

Home-cooked food served at the family table.

  • Three meals a day plus snacks
  • Menus shaped by preferences
  • Diet and texture per doctor's orders
  • Encouraging fluids and good appetite

Memory & Dementia Support

Patient, structured support for memory loss.

  • Calm, predictable routines
  • Gentle reminders and redirection
  • Visual cues around the home
  • Watchful supervision day and night

Social & Emotional Support

Connection is part of wellbeing.

  • Conversation and companionship
  • Music, games, crafts and TV time
  • Porch time on pleasant days
  • Faith and cultural preferences honored

Housekeeping & Laundry

A clean, comfortable home without the chores.

  • Daily tidying and sanitizing
  • Personal laundry and linens
  • Fresh bedding every week
  • Room kept the way residents like it

Respite Stays

A break for family caregivers, when space allows.

  • Short-term stays
  • Same care standards as long-term residents
  • Ideal after a hospital stay or during travel
  • Ask us about current availability

Comfort & End-of-Life Care

Peace, comfort and presence in the final chapter.

  • Comfort-focused personal care
  • Working alongside hospice providers
  • Family welcome to stay close
  • Quiet, dignified surroundings
Good to know

Services are written into a Negotiated Care Plan that you, your loved one and our team agree on. If needs change, we update the plan, so care always matches the person.

Care planWritten for one person, never a template
How we do it well

The details that make great care

  • We learn the person first. Routines, food likes, life story and what calms them go into the plan.
  • Consistent caregivers. Familiar faces build trust, especially for residents with memory loss.
  • Everything documented. Care, medications and changes are recorded every shift.
  • Families kept informed. You hear from us promptly about anything important.
  • Plans that keep up. When needs change, we reassess and update care right away.
