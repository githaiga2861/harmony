About Harmony Living House

Rooted in dignity, run like a family

We are a licensed adult family home in Chehalis, Washington, built on a simple belief: every person deserves to age with dignity, comfort and real human connection.

#758581Washington DSHS adult family home license
Our story

A home first, a care provider always

Harmony Living House was created for families who want more than a room and a schedule. Our home is not a medical institution. It is a house with a kitchen that smells like dinner, a living room where residents share the afternoon, and caregivers who treat each person the way they would want their own parents treated.

The home is led by provider Penninah Nyandia, who oversees daily care, staffing and every resident's plan. Our caregivers are trained, background-checked and supported to deliver care with patience, discretion and warmth.

What guides us

Mission, vision & promise

Our Mission

To provide safe, personal, around-the-clock care in a true home, so every resident can live each day with dignity and purpose.

Our Vision

To be the adult family home Lewis County families recommend first: known for honest communication and genuinely kind care.

Our Promise

We listen before we plan, keep families informed, and never stop adjusting care as your loved one's needs change.

Our values

Four things we never compromise on

Dignity

Privacy during personal care, choices respected, and every resident addressed with respect.

Safety

Supervision day and night, an emergency plan, and careful records for medications and care.

Honesty

Clear answers about care, costs and changes. Families hear from us, not just about us.

Joy

Music, conversation, sunshine on the porch and small moments that make a day good.

Licensed & accountable

Care you can trust and verify

  • Licensed and inspected by Washington State DSHS (license #758581)
  • A written Negotiated Care Plan for every resident, reviewed at least yearly and whenever needs change
  • Nurse delegation through a registered nurse for tasks that require it
  • Caregivers who are trained, background-checked and supervised
  • Residents' rights posted in the home, including how to reach the Long-Term Care Ombuds
