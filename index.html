Welcome to Harmony

Where care feels like family

Moving a parent or spouse into care is one of the biggest decisions a family makes. We built Harmony Living House to make that decision feel right: a real house on a quiet street, a small group of residents, and caregivers who know each person by name, story and smile.

  • A care plan written for one person, reviewed as needs change

Questions families ask

Quick answers

What is an adult family home?

An adult family home (AFH) is a regular house licensed by Washington State DSHS to provide room, board and personal care to a small number of adults. Residents get 24-hour supervision and help with daily living in a family-style setting.

Do you accept Medicaid?

Yes. We accept Washington Medicaid as well as private pay. Long-term care insurance and veterans' benefits may also help. See Payment & Admissions for details.

Can we visit before deciding?

Absolutely, and we encourage it. Call or message us to book a tour at a time that suits your family.

Is someone there at night?

Yes. Caregivers are in the home 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

Helpful hint

When you tour any adult family home, ask to see its Disclosure of Charges (DSHS form 15-449). It lists the home's rates, services and policies. Ours is available on request: just call or email us.
