About adult family homes

What is an adult family home?

An adult family home (AFH) is a residential house licensed by Washington State DSHS to provide room, board, laundry, supervision and personal care to a small number of adults who cannot live alone safely.

How is an AFH different from assisted living?

Adult family homes are much smaller. With only a handful of residents, care is more personal, the setting feels like a family home, and caregivers get to know each resident very well.

Is Harmony Living House licensed?

Yes. We are licensed by Washington State DSHS as an adult family home, license #758581. You can look us up on the DSHS adult family home locator.

Care & daily life

Is someone awake at night?

Caregivers are in the home 24 hours a day, seven days a week, so help is always close by.

Can you help with medications?

Yes. We give medications as prescribed, keep daily records and coordinate refills with the pharmacy. When a task requires it, a registered nurse delegates and oversees it.

Do you care for people with dementia?

We support residents living with memory loss through calm routines, reminders and close supervision. Call us to talk about your loved one's specific needs.

What about meals and special diets?

We serve home-cooked meals and snacks every day, shaped around each resident's preferences and any diet ordered by their doctor.

How will I know how my loved one is doing?

We keep families informed about health changes, appointments and anything important, and you're welcome to call or visit.

Cost & payment

Do you accept Medicaid?

Yes. We accept Washington Medicaid as well as private pay. Long-term care insurance, VA benefits and WA Cares may also help.

How much does care cost?

Private pay rates depend on the level of care. Our Disclosure of Charges (DSHS form 15-449) lists rates and what's included, and you can download it from the Payment & Admissions page.

Can someone start private pay and switch to Medicaid?

Yes. Private pay residents need at least 12 months of private funds before converting to Medicaid, as explained in our disclosure.

Visiting & moving in

When can family visit?

Families are always welcome. We simply ask that you consider residents' rest and mealtimes, and let us know if you're planning a larger gathering.

Can my loved one bring their own things?

Yes, please do. Photos, a favorite chair, blankets and keepsakes help a new room feel like home.

How soon can someone move in?

It depends on availability and paperwork. Call us and we'll tell you about current openings and timelines.

Can we tour before deciding?

Of course. Tours are by appointment. Call (951) 446-7774 or use the contact form.
